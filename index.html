// CAPABILITIES

One body. Every system. Infinite depth.

Anatoverse layers real interaction over a fully explorable anatomical model — no textbook required.

Full 3D Explorer

Orbit, zoom, and pan around a real-time rendered human figure. Click any structure to drill in.

Living Body Mode

Watch the heart beat, lungs breathe, and blood circulate in a continuously animated organism.

System Isolation

Toggle skeletal, muscular, nervous, circulatory and organ systems independently, or fade between them.

Instant Search

Type any structure — femur, optic nerve, alveoli — and the camera flies straight to it.

Adaptive Quizzes

Multiple choice, timed, and structure-labeling challenges pulled straight from what you've explored.

Progress & Achievements

Every structure explored and quiz completed builds toward streaks, badges, and certification.

Switch species instantly and compare structures side by side.
